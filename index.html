Flip

Every 7 pieces the board flips upside down. Every block falls to the new floor, so holes close by themselves and full rows clear for bonus points.

How to play

The basics

Move and rotate the falling pieces to fill complete rows. A full row clears. If the stack reaches the top, the run ends.

Hold saves a piece for later (once per piece). The outline at the bottom shows where the piece will land. Clearing 4 rows at once is a Quad; turning a T piece into a tight slot is a T-spin. Both are worth more, and doing them back to back adds a bonus.

Flip

A counter counts down 7 pieces, then the board flips upside down. Every column falls to the new floor, so gaps close. After a flip, row n from the bottom is full when every column holds at least n blocks. Blocks that have been flipped show their striped back side.

Dungeon

Each cleared row damages the monster. The box next to the monster shows its next attack and how many pieces you have left: garbage rows from below, a locked Hold, or a hidden Next queue. The boss on floor 10 flips your board. Beat a floor to pick 1 of 3 relics.

  • Fire adds 15 damage (Versus: 1 extra garbage row).
  • Ice delays the next attack by 2 pieces (Versus: blocks 2 incoming rows).
  • Thunder empties your tallest column.
  • Leaf removes 1 garbage row.

Runes trigger when the row they sit in is cleared.

Versus

Two players share one keyboard. Clearing 2, 3 or 4 rows sends 1, 2 or 4 garbage rows to the other player; T-spins, combos and back-to-back send more. Incoming garbage waits in the red meter and rises when you lock a piece without clearing. Your clears cancel it first.

Every cleared row charges your Flip meter. At 8, press your Flip key to flip your own board. Rows that fill up after the flip count as an attack. Speed rises every 30 seconds. First to win 2 rounds wins.

Controls

Click a key, then press the new one. Delete removes a second key. Esc cancels.

Some keys are used by both players. In Versus, each player needs their own keys.

Some laptop keyboards cannot register many keys pressed at the same time. If a key does nothing while your friend holds theirs, move one player to other keys.
